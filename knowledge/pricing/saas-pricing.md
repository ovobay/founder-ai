# SaaS Pricing Rules

Use exactly 3 tiers:
- Starter
- Growth
- Enterprise

Rules:
- Highlight Growth as the most popular plan
- Add a comparison table
- Show exact limits for users, usage, storage, support, and automation
- Add monthly and annual billing toggle
- Show annual savings clearly
- Add FAQs for billing, cancellation, refunds, and security
- Use strong CTAs under each plan

Avoid:
- Too many pricing tiers
- Vague feature names
- Hidden fees
- Weak CTAs like Submit or Learn More
- Long feature dumps inside pricing cards
